OphEdit: Training-Free Text-Guided Editing of Ophthalmic Surgical Video

MedViD Workshop · ECCV 2026

OphEdit is a training-free framework for text-guided editing of ophthalmic surgical videos. It uses deterministic second-order ODE inversion to capture source-video attention values, then selectively injects them into the conditional branch of classifier-free guidance during denoising—aiming to preserve fine anatomical geometry and temporal consistency while applying text-driven edits.

Read paper ↗ Official code ↗
ECCV · 2026
Method & contribution
  • Training-free, text-guided editing for ophthalmic surgical video.
  • Deterministic second-order ODE inversion to capture source-video attention Value tensors.
  • Selective attention-value injection during classifier-free-guidance denoising to retain anatomy and temporal structure.
  • Clinical evaluation includes edits such as instrument swaps and procedural variations.

SynCrash

Zero-shot accident detection & localization

A modular pipeline for detecting when an accident occurs, localizing its impact point, and classifying collision type in fixed-view traffic surveillance video without labeled real-world training data. It combines synthetic-data temporal learning with pretrained detection and physics-informed spatial reasoning.

Read paper ↗GitHub profile ↗
Method & contribution
  • VideoMAEv2-Giant fine-tuned on CARLA-based synthetic clips for temporal localization.
  • YOLO detections plus trajectory and geometric reasoning for impact localization.
  • Collision-type classification from detected vehicle configuration.
  • Reported official ACCIDENT metric: 0.38 public / 0.40 private leaderboard.
Under review

CLARIF

CT rib fracture report generation · WACV 2027 submission

CLARIF (Clinical context-Aware RIb Fracture assessment) explores clinically grounded CT report generation through anatomy-aware visual features and a context-aware decoder. The manuscript is under review; the confidential submission PDF is intentionally not linked.

Method & contribution
  • Centroid-based relative positional encoding for fracture-centric spatial reasoning.
  • Hierarchical anatomy-aware feature extraction across rib regions.
  • Clinical Context-Aware Decoder with pattern dictionaries, anatomical location encoding, and severity-aware gating.
  • Hard-negative contrastive learning to distinguish plausible but incorrect clinical descriptions.
